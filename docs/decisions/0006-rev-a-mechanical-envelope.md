# 0006: Rev A PCB and mechanical envelope

Status: Accepted; amended by 0008, 0009, 0010, and [0021](0021-rev-a-board-outline.md)

Date: 2026-10-04

## Decision

Use a four-layer PCB with rounded corners, black soldermask, accessible controls,
and a serviceable rear battery/magnet carrier. Prioritize RF clearance,
debug access, and rework over minimum size.

The original envelope was 108 × 72 mm nominal, 112 × 74 mm maximum, with a
mostly LED front and approximately 12 mm thickness target. Later records add
front buttons, dual mounting/orientation, single-sided assembly, regional
thickness targets, and the 118 × 76 mm outline.

Keep the cell free of mechanical stress and maintain antenna/coil clearances.
Fit, retention, comfort, and RF performance require the assembled carrier.
