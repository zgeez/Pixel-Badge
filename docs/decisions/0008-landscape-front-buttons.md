# 0008: Landscape wear and front-face buttons

Status: Accepted; orientation/assembly amended by [0009](0009-dual-orientation-dual-mounting.md) and [0010](0010-single-sided-assembly.md)

Date: 2026-10-06

## Decision

Place Mode and Action on the front strip below the LED field when the switch
footprints fit with board/LED clearance. Fall back to the edge directly below
the field if the strip is too small. Front tactile switches are easier to
find while wearing the badge than side controls.

The 64 mm LED field leaves little height for the controls; placement follows
the final pitch/outline. The original landscape-only and two-sided assembly
assumptions were later superseded; the button-placement rule remains.
