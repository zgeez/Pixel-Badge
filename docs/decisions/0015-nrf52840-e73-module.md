# 0015: nRF52840 module (Ebyte E73-2G4M08S1C) for Rev A

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-13
- Deciders: Project owner

## Context

[ADR 0002](0002-nrf5340-module-zephyr-platform.md) selected the nRF5340 through
the Raytac MDBT53-P1M module. On 2026-10-13, JLCPCB listed zero stock for both
MDBT53-P1M (C5796572) and its footprint-compatible MDBT53-1M (C5355663), and the
module could not be confirmed in time for the Rev A assembly order. The only
nRF5340 modules JLCPCB had in stock (Ebyte E83-2G4M03S, u-blox NORA-B120) need
an external antenna. Stock counts are a snapshot, not a reservation.

## Options considered

1. Wait for MDBT53-P1M/1M: keeps the drawn design and the nRF5340 DK evidence;
   no confirmed delivery date before the order.
2. Ebyte E83-2G4M03S (nRF5340): firmware unchanged; new footprint and an
   external antenna, a change to the integrated-antenna requirement; six in
   stock.
3. Ebyte E73-2G4M08S1C (nRF52840): ceramic antenna on the module, USB 2.0 FS,
   1 MB flash, 256 KB RAM, 32 MHz crystal and DC/DC inductors inside;
   13 × 18 × 3.0 mm, castellated pads; in stock at JLCPCB (C356849). Single
   core, fewer GPIO, new footprint and firmware port.
4. Ebyte E73-2G4M04S1B (nRF52832): in stock, but no USB peripheral, 64 KB RAM
   and 30 GPIO. Rejected.

## Decision

Use the Ebyte E73-2G4M08S1C (nRF52840) for U401 on Rev A. This replaces ADR
0002's choice of SoC and module; its NCS v3.4.1 / Zephyr platform, C++17 policy
and external 32.768 kHz crystal still apply.

The module runs in normal voltage mode (VCC = VDDH = 3V3, DCCH open). The
32.768 kHz crystal gets external 12 pF load capacitors, because the nRF52840
has no internal LFXO load; the value is calculated from the 9 pF crystal and
must be checked against the Product Specification's pin capacitance. The
29 module GPIO are all used. Rev A therefore has no spare GPIO or UART test
pads and no IMU SPI fallback, and the charger STAT output is not read by the
MCU; charger status comes from its I²C registers and INT. Analog inputs and
slow signals use the pins Ebyte marks as low-frequency I/O.

## Consequences

- The BLE controller shares the single Cortex-M4F with the application. The
  once-per-frame matrix restart can be delayed by radio interrupts; a late
  restart blanks a frame (the hardware scan-stall blanking still holds). The
  refresh under BLE load is measured on Rev A.
- Firmware needs an nRF52840 Rev A board definition and a single-image build:
  SPIM3 for the matrix (the matrix code already uses nrfx_gppi, which covers PPI
  and DPPI), UICR pin reset on P0.18 and NFC pins as GPIO. Bench results from the
  nRF5340 DK do not carry over to Rev A hardware timing or RF behavior.
- The module is 3.0 mm tall. Its board corner is a local thickness exception
  next to USB-C, like the USB-C receptacle; the carrier must clear it.
- The module sits in the bottom-right corner, antenna end toward the bottom
  edge, with a keep-out on all copper layers. Ebyte publishes no keep-out
  drawing; RF performance near the battery, magnets, USB-C shell and the
  wearer is measured on Rev A.
- The larger module moved USB-C 2.05 mm left and relocated parts in the right
  column; one CC2 sense transistor now sits in the bottom strip, away from the
  connector.
- No module footprint, clock or RF behavior has been validated before the
  order.
