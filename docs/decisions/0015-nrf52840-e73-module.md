# 0015: nRF52840 module (Ebyte E73-2G4M08S1C) for Rev A

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-10-13

## Decision

Use Ebyte E73-2G4M08S1C/nRF52840 for Rev A. The planned Raytac nRF5340 module
could not be confirmed for assembly in time. Available nRF5340 alternatives
needed an external antenna; nRF52832 lacked USB and the required memory.

The E73 keeps an integrated antenna and USB, with 1 MiB flash/256 KiB RAM, but
puts radio/application on one core. All 29 exposed GPIO are used: no UART,
spare pads, or IMU SPI fallback. Use SWD/RTT, SPIM3/PPI display timing, external
LFXO capacitors, and a dedicated nRF52840 board definition.

The larger 13 × 18 × 3 mm module changes placement and local carrier height.
Its antenna keep-out, proximity to USB/battery/magnets, clock configuration,
and combined-load timing require Rev A tests. DK results do not transfer.
